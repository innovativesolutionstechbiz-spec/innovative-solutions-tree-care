About us

Professional service, grounded in nature

We believe trees have real value and deserve informed, thoughtful care.

Our purpose

Innovative Solutions Tree Care provides professional service with tree care specialists who know the true worth of trees and value their purpose in this world.

Our philosophy brings together practical experience, respect for the natural world, and careful consideration of each property's needs.

Nature + science = balance

Trees are living systems. Their structure, growing environment, and relationship to nearby buildings, utilities, and people all matter. We aim to help customers make informed decisions about maintenance and safety.

Our values

  • Respect for trees and the environment
  • Clear communication with customers
  • Practical, property-specific solutions
  • Attention to safety and site conditions
  • Professionalism from inquiry to cleanup
